## Question Text
`6 + 6 ÷ 6 + 6 * 6 - 6 = ?`

## Options
- A) 42
- B) 37
- C) 7
- D) 1

## Correct Answer
B

## Raw Value
37

## Explanation
The correct answer is 37. Remember the order of operations (PEMDAS/BODMAS): Division and Multiplication are performed before Addition and Subtraction. So, 6 ÷ 6 = 1 and 6 * 6 = 36. The expression becomes 6 + 1 + 36 - 6, which equals 37.

## Hint
Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication/Division, Addition/Subtraction. Do them in that order!

## Trap Note
This problem tempts users to solve from left to right, ignoring the order of operations. The intuitive but incorrect answer is 42.
