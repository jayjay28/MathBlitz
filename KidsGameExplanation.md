# TurboXTable: How To Play

Hey superstar! TurboXTable is a fast math game that helps you become a multiplication master. Here is how it works:

1. A math question shows up, like `7 × 6 = ?`.
2. You type the answer on the big round buttons.
3. Watch the glowing timer:
   - The whole background changes from green to yellow to red as time passes.
   - There’s also a circle clock in the top-right corner that counts down the seconds so you always know how much time is left.
4. If you answer before the timer runs out, the screen flashes happy green and the phone gives a quick “you got it!” buzz.
5. If you miss it or run out of time, it glows red/orange and gives a “try again” buzz. You can keep going right away—no losing points.
6. After you get enough answers right in a row, you level up! Levels make the numbers bigger and the timer faster, so you keep getting better.
7. Your score goes up with every correct answer. See how high you can go!

Tips for champions:
- Say the problem out loud as you type—it helps your brain remember.
- If you are stuck, hit `C` to clear and try again.
- Keep practicing and those tricky tables will feel easy in no time.
