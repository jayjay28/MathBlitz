## Question Text
If it takes 5 machines 5 minutes to make 5 widgets, how long would it take 100 machines to make 100 widgets?

## Options
- A) 100 minutes
- B) 5 minutes
- C) 1 minute
- D) 500 minutes

## Correct Answer
B

## Raw Value
5

## Explanation
The correct answer is 5 minutes. The problem states that 5 machines make 5 widgets in 5 minutes. This establishes a rate: it takes 1 machine 5 minutes to make 1 widget. Therefore, 100 machines working in parallel can produce 100 widgets in that same 5-minute timeframe.

## Hint
Think about the output rate of a single machine.

## Trap Note
This is a classic rate problem. The intuitive "System 1" brain wants to create a simple linear relationship (5/5/5 -> 100/100/100), but the logic is that the rate of production per machine is the constant.
