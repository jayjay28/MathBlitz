## Question Text
A jacket is on sale for 20% off its original price. You have a coupon that gives you an additional 20% off the sale price. What is the total percentage discount from the original price?

## Options
- A) 40%
- B) 36%
- C) 44%
- D) 38%

## Correct Answer
B

## Raw Value
36

## Explanation
The correct answer is 36%. The second discount of 20% applies to the *already discounted* price, not the original price. If the jacket costs $100, the first 20% discount makes it $80. The second 20% discount is 20% of $80 ($16), making the final price $64. This is a total discount of $36, or 36% off the original $100.

## Hint
The second discount is a percentage of a smaller number.

## Trap Note
This problem exploits the common heuristic of simply adding percentages together (20% + 20% = 40%). It forces the user to engage "System 2" to realize the base for the second percentage calculation has changed.
